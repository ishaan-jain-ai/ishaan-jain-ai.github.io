Things I’ve built / Ensemble Health Partners

From a chatbot MVP to agents for 5,000+ people

A small RAG chatbot that turned into an organisation-wide agent, and the Copilot agents that followed.

Where
Ensemble Health Partners
Roles
Senior Analytics Associate, 2022 to 2024 (the MVP); Lead, Analytics, 2024 to 2026 (the agents)
Reach
5,000+ users for the SOP and policy agent; 20+ vendors for business continuity
Stack
LangChain, Pinecone, Copilot Studio, Power Automate, Microsoft Graph, Azure AI Foundry

At Ensemble Health Partners I led a three-person team that built a RAG chatbot MVP. Several departments adopted it, and it was later developed into an SOP and policy agent rolled out to the whole organisation. As Lead, Analytics I went on to lead development of 5+ enterprise Microsoft Copilot agents.

The problem

Staff needed quick, reliable answers from internal SOPs and policies, and several teams had routine work that could be automated, such as business-continuity analysis across vendors.

What I built

The MVP

The MVP was a retrieval-augmented chatbot built on LangChain and Pinecone. It improved response accuracy and cut query-resolution time by 40%, and several departments adopted it. It was later developed into the organisation-wide SOP and policy agent.

Copilot Studio or custom code

Agents were built in Copilot Studio, with Power Automate flows and Microsoft Graph, or with custom logic on Azure AI Foundry, depending on the use case. Copilot Studio is quick to build in and sits inside Microsoft 365, where staff already work; custom logic on Azure AI Foundry is for agents that need more control than low-code flows give.

Rollout

The SOP and policy agent was rolled out to the whole organisation, more than 5,000 users. The business-continuity analysis agent, built in Copilot Studio, covered 20+ vendors. Across the agents, time spent on the automated workflows fell by 20% on average.

Trade-offs

Low-code where it fits. Copilot Studio meant faster delivery inside tools staff already use. It gives less control than custom code, so agents with more complex logic were built on Azure AI Foundry.

An MVP before an organisation-wide rollout. We had measured value, 40% faster query resolution, and real users before scaling, at the price of a slower path to company-wide reach.
