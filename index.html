Not proven

The judge has v2 winning 74.4% of 80 items (ties count half; 95% interval 65.9–82.9%).

The judge favours v2 by 24 points and its interval excludes a tie, but that isn’t enough. In my study, on pairs people saw as ties, judges went up to 26 points from a tie and named a winner on 5 to 7 of 15 pairs. More test items won’t fix this; human labels will. Label 25 items picked at random, without looking at the judge’s verdicts, and check again.
